For the past five months, Starry Steam has been actively working on a new version of the Zombie Plague gamemode, while also adopting the name for itself, as the original creator of the gamemode, The Fire Fuchs, gave us permission to continue his work and consider his version obsolete. Today, on Halloween, the gamemode is officially released as version 1.0.

We plan to continue developing this version of Zombie Plague and polishing it towards an ideal state as our free time allows, while organizing gaming events to test changes along the way. It is intended to become the primary release that Garry's Mod players will associate with this gamemode concept.

The new Zombie Plague features numerous changes that improve replayability and make draws impossible in many situations. The gamemode has lost its classes, but gained an expanded Extra Items shop and melee weapons that alter the player's attributes on the Human team. Basic bot support has been added, and the gamemode now comes with three recommended and exclusive maps included in its content. And, of course, a great deal of work has gone into optimizing the downloadable content, resulting in a gamemode that weighs only 10 megabytes.

Alongside the gamemode, a ten-minute analytical video is being released, presenting the community's general thoughts on defining gamemodes that could be considered counterparts to Zombie Plague.

The video is presented in Ukrainian and fully dubbed into Russian and English. The Zombie Plague gamemode itself, in turn, has been translated into every language available in Garry's Mod using artificial intelligence.
